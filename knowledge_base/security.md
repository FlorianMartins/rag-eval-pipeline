# Security and Compliance

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

Northwind Cloud is certified ISO 27001 and completes a SOC 2 Type II audit every year.

Customer data is hosted in the Frankfurt and Paris regions and never leaves the European Union.

Single sign-on is available through SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect on the Team and Enterprise plans.

Security incidents are reported to affected customers within 72 hours.
