# Billing and Plans

Northwind Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Team and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs 12 euros per user per month and includes 50 GB of storage.

The Team plan costs 29 euros per user per month and includes 1 TB of shared storage.

Enterprise pricing is negotiated with the sales team and includes a dedicated account manager.

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and are payable within 30 days.

Customers who pay annually receive a 15 percent discount on every plan.
